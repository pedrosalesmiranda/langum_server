- число vs номер
- Сейчас vs Теперь
- sorry (variants)
--------------------------------------------------------
Сейчас – most common for “right now” or the current moment.
Теперь – used for “from now on” or when contrasting past and present.
Example:
I’m busy now → Я сейчас занят.
Now things are different → Теперь всё по-другому.
---------------------------------------------------------
SORRY (variants)
Russian Phrase	Pronunciation	Meaning/Use	Formality / Context
Извини́ / Извините	izviní / izviníte	"Sorry" / "Excuse me"	Informal / Formal apology or getting attention
Прости́ / Простите	prostí / prostíte	"Forgive me" / "Sorry" (asking for forgiveness)	Informal / Formal apology, more personal
Мне жаль	mnye zhal'	"I'm sorry" / "I feel sorry" (expressing regret or sympathy)	Neutral, expressing empathy or regret
Сожале́ю	sozháleyu	"I regret" / "I’m sorry" (formal, serious regret)	Formal or serious apologies or expressions of regret
